The Founder's Message

"At Waaree, our vision has always been clear — to harness the power of the sun to build a brighter, cleaner, and more self-reliant future for India and the world. What began as a bold ambition has now evolved into a nationwide movement driving energy independence and environmental stewardship.

Together, we're not just creating solar solutions
— we're igniting a legacy of impact."

Experience

Rajender Mohan Malla has been associated with our Company since 2019. He has previously held the position of a director in various companies, such as SIDBI Venture Capital Limited, IDBI Capital Markets and Securities Limited and IDBI Asset Management Limited.

Education

Rajender Mohan Malla holds a bachelor's degree in commerce and a master's degree in business administration from the University of Delhi. He is also a certified associate of Indian Institute of Bankers.
